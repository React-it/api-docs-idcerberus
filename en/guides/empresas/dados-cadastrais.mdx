# Business registration data

Use these services to validate whether a company exists, enrich CNPJ data,
and query official registration information. They help with PJ onboarding
flows, registration analysis, database updates, fraud prevention, and
operational validation before opening a business relationship.

All services in this category are consumed through the endpoint:

```http
POST /api/service-api
```

The `service` field defines which query runs.

## When to use it

Use this category when you need to:

1. confirm a CNPJ's registration status at the Federal Revenue;
2. retrieve the official name, trade name, opening date, activities, and legal nature;
3. check state registration in SINTEGRA;
4. query extended addresses associated with the CNPJ;
5. query digital presence, domains, and online operation signals;
6. query periods, invoices, and barcodes for DAS MEI or Simples Nacional - MEI collection;
7. track the company's registration change history, share capital, and tax regime;
8. query phone numbers, merchant category (MCC), marketplace presence, online ads, and the company's reputation on review platforms;
9. query investment fund data linked to the CNPJ and the time evolution of capital, employees, and business owners.

## Available services

| Service | `service` code | Main use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PJ data enrichment | `SERVICE_CORPORATE_DATA_ENRICHMENT` | Returns the company's full registration, including official name, trade name, economic activities, legal nature, regime, and capital |
| CNPJ status at the Federal Revenue | `SERVICE_RFB_PJ` | Queries the CNPJ's registration status and the status date at the Federal Revenue |
| CNPJ at the Federal Revenue on-demand | `SERVICE_RFB_PJ_ON_DEMAND` | Fetches or updates the company's registration data at request time |
| SINTEGRA | `SERVICE_SINTEGRA_CONSULTATION` | Queries state registration data in SINTEGRA, including state, status, and CFDF when applicable |
| Extended business addresses | `SERVICE_ADDRESSES_EXTENDED_CNPJ` | Returns addresses associated with the CNPJ, including street, neighborhood, city, state, country, postal code, and address type |
| PJ electoral donations | `SERVICE_ELECTORAL_DONORS_CNPJ` | Queries electoral donations made by the company and aggregated statistics |
| PJ electoral service providers | `SERVICE_ELECTORAL_PROVIDERS_CNPJ` | Queries electoral service provision associated with the CNPJ |
| DAS MEI at the Federal Revenue | `SERVICE_DAS_MEI` | Queries DAS MEI periods, assessment status, invoice, and barcode |
| Simples Nacional - MEI collection | `SERVICE_PGMEI` | Queries the MEI's DAS with monthly collection history and payment status |
| Basic data history | `SERVICE_HISTORY_BASIC_DATA` | Queries the CNPJ's basic registration change history: name, tax regime, registration status, CNAE, and share capital |
| Merchant category | `SERVICE_MERCHANT_CATEGORY_DATA` | Queries the company's MCC categorization, by direct association with Abecs or inferred from the CNAE |
| Phone numbers | `SERVICE_PHONES_EXTENDED_COMPANY` | Queries phone numbers associated with the company, with validity, priority, and source indicators |
| Company evolution | `SERVICE_COMPANY_EVOLUTION` | Queries the time evolution of capital, employees, branches, and business owners, with growth trend |
| Marketplaces | `SERVICE_MARKETPLACE_DATA` | Queries the company's presence in marketplaces, stores operated, products listed, and ratings |
| Online ads | `SERVICE_ONLINE_ADS` | Queries online ads linked to the company on classifieds portals and peer-to-peer marketplaces |
| Ratings and reputation | `SERVICE_REPUTATIONS_AND_REVIEWS` | Queries the company's reputation across different review platforms, with a consolidated view and history |
| Investment fund data | `SERVICE_INVESTMENT_FUND_DATA` | Queries registration and operational information for investment funds associated with the CNPJ, per CVM |

## Quick example

Business registration enrichment query:

```bash
curl --location '{base_url}/api/service-api' \
  --header 'Authorization: Bearer {jwt_token}' \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data '{
    "service": "SERVICE_CORPORATE_DATA_ENRICHMENT",
    "cnpj": "cnpj"
  }'
```

Payload:

```json
{
  "service": "SERVICE_CORPORATE_DATA_ENRICHMENT",
  "cnpj": "cnpj"
}
```

Expected response:

```json
{
  "result": {
    "cnpj": "00000000000000",
    "status": "ATIVA",
    "name": "REACT IT SOLUCOES EM TECNOLOGIA LTDA",
    "fantasyName": "REACT IT SOLUTIONS",
    "origin": "Receita Federal",
    "regime": "SIMPLES",
    "foundedDate": "2018-04-04T00:00:00Z"
  },
  "status": {
    "code": 200,
    "message": "Success"
  }
}
```

## Best practices

1. Send the CNPJ without a mask when possible, keeping only digits.
2. Use data enrichment when you need to build a complete registration view of the company.
3. Use the CNPJ status when you only need to validate whether the company is active or in good standing.
4. For companies with state-level operations, combine Federal Revenue and SINTEGRA.
