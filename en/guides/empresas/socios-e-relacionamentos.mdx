# Business owners and relationships

This category brings together queries to understand who's behind a
company, what ties exist between people and CNPJs, and what risks show up
around the ownership structure. It's useful for PJ onboarding, ultimate
beneficial owner analysis, business owner KYC, economic relationship
assessment, and investigating relevant ties.

All services in this category are consumed through the endpoint:

```http
POST /api/service-api
```

## When to use it

Use this category when you need to:

1. identify owners, representatives, and a company's relationships;
2. map individuals related to the CNPJ at the first level;
3. query the full corporate/management ownership structure (QSA) of the headquarters at the Federal Revenue;
4. map the economic group and infer the ownership structure's influence level;
5. assess legal cases associated with business owners, individually or aggregated;
6. query PEP, sanctions, and compliance history indicators for business owners.

## Available services

| Service | `service` code | Main use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company relationships | `SERVICE_COMPANY_RELATIONSHIP` | Returns the company's relationships, including owners, employees, owned companies, and relationships with people |
| Business owners at the Federal Revenue | `SERVICE_COMPANY_RFB_OWNERS` | Returns registration data for business owners identified at the Federal Revenue |
| First-level partners | `SERVICE_FIRST_LEVEL_PARTNER` | Summarizes circles of individuals related to the CNPJ, with metrics on age, income, location, lawsuits, and distributions |
| Federal Revenue - QSA | `SERVICE_RF_QSA` | Queries the CNPJ's corporate/management ownership structure, with the headquarters' registration data and the list of business owners and administrators |
| Economic group relationships | `SERVICE_ECONOMIC_GROUP_RELATIONSHIPS` | Queries entities (people and companies) that are part of the same economic group as the CNPJ, with current and historical relationships |
| Business ownership influence | `SERVICE_OWNERS_INFLUENCE` | Infers the ownership structure's influence level, considering media exposure, political involvement, and business owners' lawsuits |
| Business owners' lawsuit distribution | `SERVICE_OWNERS_LAWSUITS_DISTRIBUTION` | Queries aggregated data on the distribution of lawsuits the company's business owners are involved in |
| Business owners' legal cases | `SERVICE_JURIDICAL_PROCESSES_PJ_OWNERS` | Queries cases associated with business owners and groups indicators by document |
| Business owners' KYC and compliance | `SERVICE_COMPANY_KYC_OWNERS` | Queries PEP exposure, current and historical sanctions, and relevant occurrences within the ownership structure |
| Business owners' electoral donations | `SERVICE_OWNERS_ELECTORAL_DONORS_CNPJ` | Queries electoral donations made by business owners linked to the CNPJ |

## Quick example

Company relationships query:

```bash
curl --location '{base_url}/api/service-api' \
  --header 'Authorization: Bearer {jwt_token}' \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data '{
    "service": "SERVICE_COMPANY_RELATIONSHIP",
    "cnpj": "cnpj"
  }'
```

Payload:

```json
{
  "service": "SERVICE_COMPANY_RELATIONSHIP",
  "cnpj": "cnpj"
}
```

Expected response:

```json
{
  "result": {
    "cnpj": "00000000000000",
    "relationshipTotal": 1,
    "totalOwners": 1,
    "totalEmployees": 1,
    "companyRelationships": [
      {
        "cpf": "77272722314",
        "name": "BUSINESS OWNER NAME",
        "relationshipName": "SOCIO-ADMINISTRADOR",
        "origin": "RECEITA FEDERAL"
      }
    ]
  },
  "status": {
    "code": 200,
    "message": "Success"
  }
}
```

## How to combine services

A PJ analysis usually starts with `SERVICE_COMPANY_RELATIONSHIP` to
understand the company's structure. Next, `SERVICE_FIRST_LEVEL_PARTNER`
helps summarize the ownership surroundings. When you need to dig deeper
into risk, use `SERVICE_JURIDICAL_PROCESSES_PJ_OWNERS` and
`SERVICE_COMPANY_KYC_OWNERS`.

For fields, full examples, and responses, see the
[API Reference](/api-reference/autorização/gerar-token-de-api) or the
[Business technical catalog](/guides/servicos-pessoa-juridica).
