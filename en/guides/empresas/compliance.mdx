# Business compliance

Business compliance services help identify financial, regulatory, and
reputational risks tied to CNPJs. They can be used in PJ onboarding,
vendor monitoring, third-party assessment, credit processes, fraud
prevention, and AML/CFT routines.

All services in this category are consumed through the endpoint:

```http
POST /api/service-api
```

## When to use it

Use this category when you need to:

1. check a company's active debts with government agencies;
2. query protests at notary offices;
3. assess public exposure and news related to business owners;
4. assess exposure and compliance of companies related to the betting market;
5. identify ultimate beneficial owners and equity stake percentages;
6. query federal and state negative certificates (PGFN, CGU, CNJ, state debts, PCD, Simples Nacional);
7. assess aggregated KYC for the company's full economic group;
8. complement a registration analysis with risk and restriction signals.

## Available services

| Service | `service` code | Main use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PJ active debts | `SERVICE_ACTIVE_DEBT_PJ` | Queries the company's active debts with the government and returns totals, sources, and a list of debts |
| PJ negative protest certificate | `SERVICE_PROTEST_PJ` | Queries protests at notary offices and returns title details found |
| Business owners' media exposure and profile | `SERVICE_MEDIA_PROFILE_EXPOSURE_PJ` | Queries public exposure, notoriety, unpopularity, and news related to the company's business owners |
| PJ betting compliance | `SERVICE_COMPLIANCE_BET_PJ` | Queries sports exposure, betting relationship, registration data, and regulatory signals for companies in the sector |
| FGTS | `SERVICE_FGTS` | Queries the employer's FGTS compliance certificate |
| Labor lawsuits | `SERVICE_LABOR_LAWSUITS` | Queries an on-demand certificate stating whether there are labor lawsuits related to the company |
| Legal case distribution | `SERVICE_LAWSUITS_DISTRIBUTION_DATA_COMPANY` | Queries aggregated data on the distribution of lawsuits the company is involved in |
| Employee KYC and compliance | `SERVICE_EMPLOYEES_KYC` | Queries KYC and regulatory compliance indicators for employees, including PEP status and national and international sanctions |
| Union agreements | `SERVICE_SYNDICATE_AGREEMENTS` | Queries the union agreements signed between the company and the unions that represent its employees |
| PJ credit score | `SERVICE_QUOD_CREDIT_SCORE_COMPANY` | Queries a business's credit score by CNPJ, with level and risk classification |
| PJ multi-data credit score | `SERVICE_BOAVISTA_ONE_SCORE_COMPANY` | Queries a business's multi-data credit score by CNPJ |
| PJ restrictive data | `SERVICE_BOAVISTA_CREDIT_SCORE_COMPANY` | Queries a business's restrictive credit data, with an indicator and count of restrictions |
| PJ negative flags | `SERVICE_QUOD_CREDIT_RISK_COMPANY` | Queries a business's negative credit flags, with level and risk classification |
| PJ Quantum credit score | `SERVICE_QUANTUM_CUSTOM_SCORE_COMPANY` | Queries a business's Quantum credit score by CNPJ |
| Ultimate beneficial owners | `SERVICE_ULTIMATE_BENEFICIAL_OWNERS` | Identifies the company's ultimate beneficial owners, with an accumulated equity stake percentage through indirect chains |
| Equity stake percentage | `SERVICE_BOAVISTA_OWNER_PARTICIPATION_DATA_COMPANY` | Queries the equity stake percentage of each of the company's business owners |
| PGFN debts | `SERVICE_PGFN_COMPANY` | Queries a certificate for federal tax debts and active union debt at the PGFN |
| PCD quota | `SERVICE_PCD_COMPANY` | Queries a certificate confirming compliance with the legal PCD quota and rehabilitated beneficiaries |
| CGU negative certificate | `SERVICE_CGU_NEGATIVE_CERTIFICATE_COMPANY` | Queries active sanctions in CEIS, CNEP, and CEPIM |
| CNJ negative certificate | `SERVICE_CNJ_NEGATIVE_CERTIFICATE_COMPANY` | Queries civil convictions for administrative misconduct and ineligibility |
| State debt negative certificate | `SERVICE_STATE_DEBT_CERTIFICATE_COMPANY` | Queries state debts, available for every state |
| Simples Nacional status | `SERVICE_SIMPLES_COMPANY` | Queries enrollment status in Simples Nacional and SIMEI |
| Economic group KYC and compliance | `SERVICE_ECONOMIC_GROUP_KYC_COMPANY` | Queries aggregated PEP and sanctions indicators for the full economic group |

## Quick example

PJ betting compliance query:

```bash
curl --location '{base_url}/api/service-api' \
  --header 'Authorization: Bearer {jwt_token}' \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data '{
    "service": "SERVICE_COMPLIANCE_BET_PJ",
    "cnpj": "cnpj"
  }'
```

Payload:

```json
{
  "service": "SERVICE_COMPLIANCE_BET_PJ",
  "cnpj": "cnpj"
}
```

Expected response:

```json
{
  "result": {
    "cnpj": "00000000000000",
    "status": "ATIVA",
    "origin": "Receita Federal",
    "officialName": "ANONYMIZED OFFICIAL NAME",
    "tradeName": "ANONYMIZED TRADE NAME",
    "isBet": false,
    "onlineBettingCompliance": []
  },
  "status": {
    "code": 200,
    "message": "Consulta de API Realizada com Sucesso"
  }
}
```

## Reading the results

The `status` object indicates the query's technical result. Business
signals live in the `result` object, such as debt amounts, protests,
relationships, regulatory exposure, and compliance indicators. In critical
analyses, combine these services with registration data and ownership
relationships.

For fields, full examples, and responses, see the
[API Reference](/en/api-reference/autorização/gerar-token-de-api) or the
[Business technical catalog](/en/guides/servicos-pessoa-juridica).
